# 给 Gemini：两位主角的立绘、表情，加五张新背景

日期：2026-11-04。Claude 起草。人设见 `../设定集/人物.md`。

## 用法（给你看的）

- **雅娜**（转化与化归，原来的“阿化”）：就用你挑定的立绘 A（桌面 `image.png`），不重画。这一轮只出表情差分（第 3 条）。叉腰的 B 留着当“得意”的姿势。
- **塔莉**（整体代换，原来的“阿替”）：还没有图。先出立绘（第 1 条），挑一张最满意的，再出表情差分（第 3 条）。
- 两人都多了一个表情，剧情里的低谷要用：雅娜“动摇”，塔莉“低落”。
- **新背景 5 张**（第 4 条）：日常剧情要去集市、河边、菜园、溪上木桥和遗迹深处。
- 名字还没定也不影响出图：提示词里的英文名只是给模型的称呼。
- 老规矩：
  - 不上传学生照片或作业；
  - 公开时标注“AI 生成（Gemini）”；
  - 用之前确认条款允许公开、可商用。
- **交回**：塔莉挑中的立绘、两人的表情差分、5 张背景，放桌面告诉我就行。我来抠图、压缩、接进小样。

## 第 1 条 · 塔莉立绘（附上雅娜的立绘 A 当画风参考）

她是队里最小、最吵、跑得最快的一个。随身一包**空白**小纸签，用的时候才写；手里总拎着一个布包袱：反复出现的东西，她就打成一包。名录院嫌她乱给守卫起外号，把她的纸签全扔了。

```text
Use the same art style, line weight, coloring and framing as the attached image.
Visual novel (galgame) character sprite of an original character: Tally, a cheerful, quick, warm-hearted girl about 16, the youngest and loudest of her travel group. Her power is wrapping a repeated chunk into one bundle and tying a short new name tag on it.
Design: a canvas messenger satchel stuffed with small blank paper tags on strings (every tag is completely blank); an amber cloth bundle tied with a knot on top, held up in one hand; long chestnut-brown hair in a high ponytail tied with a blank paper tag; a light cream travel shirt with sleeves rolled to the elbows, an amber neckerchief, a short olive vest, practical trousers and ankle boots; warm brown eyes; a bright open smile with eyes slightly curved; energetic stance, leaning forward a little. Accent color: amber.
Anime illustration, clean lineart, soft cel shading with gentle gradients, polished commercial visual-novel quality.
Knee-up standing pose, body turned slightly to one side, face toward the viewer, character centered, head to knees fully in frame, portrait 2:3.
Solid flat chroma-green background (#00FF00) with no floor shadow, for easy cutout.
The character is high-school age (about 16–17). Modest, practical, school-appropriate clothing; non-sexualized; no revealing outfit; no suggestive pose.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no symbols, no logos, no watermark.
```

多出几张，挑的时候看三样：
- 纸签是空白的；
- 和雅娜站在一起像同一个游戏里的人；
- 一眼看得出她爱打包、爱贴签。

## 第 3 条 · 表情差分（在各自挑中的那张上改）

每次只改一种表情，把 `[表情]` 换成下表里的英文。

```text
Edit the attached image. Keep exactly the same character, face shape, hair, outfit, pose, framing and green background. Change only the facial expression to: [表情].
No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark.
```

| 角色 | 表情（中文 → 填进去的英文） |
|---|---|
| 雅娜 | 平静 `calm, neutral`；微笑 `small warm smile`；思考 `thinking, eyes looking aside, hand near chin`；惊讶 `surprised, eyes wide`；得意 `confident smirk`；**动摇** `shaken and uncertain, brows drawn together, eyes lowered` |
| 塔莉 | 平静 `calm, neutral`；大笑 `big cheerful laugh, eyes closed`；思考 `thinking, head tilted, finger on cheek`；惊讶 `surprised, mouth open`；着急 `flustered, slightly panicked`；**低落** `downcast, sad eyes looking down, small frown` |

## 第 4 条 · 五张新背景（16:9）

都不要人物，也不要文字，招牌、石头、墙上一律空白。

```text
Visual novel background art, 16:9, anime style, detailed and painterly, soft lighting, no characters, no text, no letters, no numbers, no symbols, no logos, no watermark.
Scene: [场景].
Muted palette of slate teal, warm sand and aged brass.
```

`[场景]` 依次换成：

1. **集市**（第 2、5、12 集）：`a small open-air market at the edge of a misty grassland in the morning: a few wooden stalls with baskets of vegetables and fruit, a hanging balance scale, cloth awnings, no signs`
2. **河边**（第 6 集）：`a quiet riverbank with smooth stones and reeds in soft morning mist; one flat stone at the water's edge has a single horizontal scratch line on it`
3. **菜园**（第 7、10 集）：`a small vegetable garden next to a camp, neat rows of young plants, a freshly dug shallow water channel running through a gentle dip in the ground, a wooden watering can`
4. **溪上木桥**（第 4、8 集）：`a narrow wooden footbridge over a small creek in a grassland, one plank clearly missing in the middle, a spare plank lying on the bank`
5. **遗迹深处**（第 14—17 集）：`an underground stone hall reached by worn stone steps, faint light falling from above, an empty stone table in the center, walls covered with rows of short carved strokes that are not letters or numbers`
