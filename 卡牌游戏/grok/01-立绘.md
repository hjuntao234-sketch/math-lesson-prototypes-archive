# 给 Grok：罗盘指挥官 · 立绘试做

日期：2026-11-04。Claude 起草，用户拿去给 Grok 试。

这一轮只试 4 个角色和 1 张场景图。目的是先把**画风**定下来，不追求一次出齐。

## 怎么用

1. **先出群像（第 0 条）**，四个人站一排，用来定画风。挑一张最喜欢的。
2. **再逐个出单人立绘（第 1—4 条）**。每条都已经带着同一段“风格”和“安全”要求，不用另外加。
   - Grok 如果能“以图改图”，就把第 1 步选中的群像当参考图一起发，在提示词最后加一句：`Keep the same art style as the reference image.`
3. **满意的角色再出表情（第 5 条）**：同一人四个表情，对话时用。
4. **最后出场景主视觉（第 6 条）和主角背影（第 7 条）**。

提示词用英文写，生图模型通常更吃英文；每条上面有中文说明。

**交回给我的东西**：你挑中的图（每人 1—2 张），加一句你喜欢或不喜欢哪里。我按下面的检查单看一遍，再放进小样。

**注意**：
- 不要上传任何学生照片或学生的作业。
- 项目要公开，以后按 CC BY-SA 发布、可商用。正式用之前，先确认 Grok 的使用条款允许生成图公开发布和商用。
- 公开时，每张图都标注“AI 生成（Grok）”，改过的写“AI 生成，人工修改”。这既是对看的人诚实，也是国内的要求：2025 年 9 月起施行的《人工智能生成合成内容标识办法》要求公开传播的 AI 生成内容做标识。
- 林予衡这一轮不动，她在世界里当什么由你定。

## 第 1 条 · 阿化（转化与化归）

设计来源是学法原文：“换个样子，还是同一道题。”所以给她一件**正反两面都能穿的斗篷**，和一把**转动用的钥匙**。她被名录院嫌“招数太土”，表情是被小看过、但心里有数的半笑。

```text
Original character design: Ahua, a calm, practical girl about 17. Her power is turning a problem into a different-looking but equal problem.
Design motifs: a reversible hooded short cloak, one edge flipped so both sides are visible (slate-teal outside, warm sand lining); a brass turning-key tool hanging from her belt; fingerless gloves; simple travel boots.
Short wavy dark hair, steady eyes, a faint confident half-smile, like someone who was underestimated but knows what she can do.
Accent color: deep teal.
Style: anime-inspired illustration, clean confident line art, soft cel shading, natural proportions (not chibi), muted palette of slate teal, warm sand and aged brass, full-body standing pose, three-quarter view, plain light gray background for easy cutout, soft even lighting.
High-school age, modest, practical, school-appropriate clothing; non-sexualized; no revealing outfit; no suggestive pose.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no symbols, no logos, no watermark.
```

## 第 2 条 · 阿替（整体代换）

学法原文：“一块反复出现，就整块来算。”还有她自己的台词：“他们嫌我只会给东西改名字。”所以她的道具是**一包空白名牌**和一个**用布打好的包裹**。名牌一定是空白的，不让 AI 乱写字。

```text
Design motifs: a messenger satchel full of blank paper tags on strings (every tag is blank); a small cloth-wrapped bundle tied with a blank tag, held up in one hand; long hair in a high ponytail tied with a blank paper tag; light layered travel clothes with rolled sleeves.
Energetic stance, bright eyes, open smile.
Accent color: amber.
Style: anime-inspired illustration, clean confident line art, soft cel shading, natural proportions (not chibi), muted palette of slate teal, warm sand and aged brass, full-body standing pose, three-quarter view, plain light gray background for easy cutout, soft even lighting.
High-school age, modest, practical, school-appropriate clothing; non-sexualized; no revealing outfit; no suggestive pose.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no symbols, no logos, no watermark.
```

## 第 3 条 · 阿构（构造）

```text
Original character design: Agou, a quiet, steady girl about 17, slightly taller than her friends. Her power is adding the missing piece that connects what you have to what you need.
Design motifs: a canvas work apron over simple clothes; a tool belt with a folding wooden ruler and a drawing compass (the geometry tool); a few short wooden rods tucked under one arm, like spare parts for a small bridge; braided hair pinned up; sleeves rolled to the elbow.
Practical, gentle expression, calm posture.
Accent color: brick red.
Style: anime-inspired illustration, clean confident line art, soft cel shading, natural proportions (not chibi), muted palette of slate teal, warm sand and aged brass, full-body standing pose, three-quarter view, plain light gray background for easy cutout, soft even lighting.
High-school age, modest, practical, school-appropriate clothing; non-sexualized; no revealing outfit; no suggestive pose.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no symbols, no logos, no watermark.
```

## 第 4 条 · 白名（对手，名录院首席学徒）

他只背名字，不看东西本身。所以**眼睛总是看着书**，书用链子锁在腰上，挂着空白书签。他是对手，不是坏人：要体面、骄傲，有一点僵。

```text
Original character design: Baiming, a proud young man about 17, top apprentice of an academy that teaches only by memorizing names. He is a rival, not a villain.
Design motifs: an immaculate white high-collared long coat with black trim; a thick closed catalog book chained to his belt, with several blank ribbon bookmarks hanging from it; white gloves; neat silver-white hair.
His gaze is lowered toward his book instead of looking at the world; elegant but stiff posture; a cool, slightly superior expression.
Accent color: cold silver.
Style: anime-inspired illustration, clean confident line art, soft cel shading, natural proportions (not chibi), muted palette of slate teal, warm sand and aged brass, full-body standing pose, three-quarter view, plain light gray background for easy cutout, soft even lighting.
High-school age, modest, school-appropriate clothing; non-sexualized; no suggestive pose.
```

## 第 6 条 · 场景主视觉：不等式原野（16:9）

地图页和标题页的背景。四条路从营地出发，伸进迷雾。迷雾就是“还没看懂的地方”。远处是下一章的森林。石碑上不能有字。

```text
Wide 16:9 landscape key art for an educational strategy card game: a misty grassland plain at dawn.
Four dirt paths fan out from a small campfire camp in the foreground toward distant areas hidden in thick fog.
Ancient weathered stone tablets and a broken stone arch half-buried in the grass, with no carvings or writing.
Far beyond the fog, the dark edge of a forest.
Hopeful, quiet mood. Muted palette of slate teal and warm sand with aged-brass highlights. Soft painterly anime background style.
No people except tiny distant silhouettes. No text, no letters, no numbers, no symbols, no logos, no watermark.
```

## 第 7 条 · 主角背影

主角就是玩家“你”，从来不露脸，不定性别。只画背影。

```text
The player character seen only from behind: a figure in a long dark travel coat with the hood down, standing at a wooden map table in a small camp at dawn, holding a brass compass. The face is never shown; gender is not identifiable.
Beyond the camp, mist covers a wide grassland with four paths leading away.
Anime-inspired illustration, soft cel shading, muted slate-teal and warm sand palette with brass highlights.
```

## 我拿到图以后会查的

- **人设干净**：年龄看起来是高中生；衣服得体，没有暴露和暗示性的姿势。
- **原创**：不像任何现成作品里的角色。
- **能看出她做什么**：不看名字，只看道具，能不能猜到她擅长什么（费曼那块瓦片）。
- **画风统一**：四个人放在一起像同一个游戏。
- **图里没有乱码字**：名牌、书签、石碑都是空白的。
- **能抠图**：背景是纯色，能放进卡牌框。
